Our Story

The Campus Event Management System (CEMS) was born from a simple observation: organizing and attending campus events shouldn't be complicated. Founded in 2024 by a group of passionate computer science students, CEMS has grown into the university's central hub for all event-related activities.

We noticed that students often missed out on exciting events simply because information was scattered across notice boards, email chains, and social media groups. Our mission was to create a single, accessible platform that brings all campus events together in one place.

Today, CEMS serves over 5,000 students across 30+ departments, hosting everything from academic seminars and career fairs to cultural festivals and sports tournaments.
