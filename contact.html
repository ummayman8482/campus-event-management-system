Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I register for an event?

Simply navigate to the Events page, find an event you're interested in, and click "Register Now." You'll be taken to the registration form where you can fill in your details.

Can I cancel my registration?

Yes, you can cancel your registration up to 48 hours before the event. After that, cancellations may not be possible depending on event capacity.

Is CEMS free to use?

Absolutely! CEMS is completely free for all students, faculty, and staff. Some events may have their own fees, but the platform itself is free.

How do I become an event organizer?

Contact our admin team through this form or email cems@university.edu. We'll guide you through the process of becoming a verified event organizer on the platform.
